Sketches for the speeches website

Design sketches for the website that will accompany From Warfare to Welfare: Leaders, Eras, and the Evolution of Policy Attention, by Oscar Calvo-González, Axel Eizmendi and Germán Reyes. Click a page to open it. Pages marked Interactive respond to clicks and typing. Each page has a button at the lower right that returns to this list.

The numbers on these pages come from preliminary versions of the data and the topic model. They will change before the release.

Chosen direction

The home page starts with the paper. Below the paper is the weave, followed by buttons to Words and Topics. Every download button opens the download page. The four pages share one menu and the typefaces Piazzolla and Archivo.

Earlier sketches: six ideas

Ideas 1 to 3. The weave shows every message as one colored line. The word time machine counts any word across two centuries and downloads every passage that uses it. The atlas plays the leading topic of each country decade by decade and downloads that decade.

Ideas 4 to 6. A quiz built from real quotes. A download builder that shows the size of a selection before you download it. Questions answered only with quotes from the messages, each one exportable.

Foundations: pages every version needs

Paper first. The home page opens with the paper, its main figure, one quote for each of the four eras, and the download. A presidents page shows each president's topic shares. The phone view shows the Spanish version.

Archive first. A grid of every country and year in the collection, colored by length or by how the message was delivered. Each mark opens a speech page with the text, the source scan, the topic shares and the messages of the same year.
